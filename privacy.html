Privacy Policy

Clipping Streams ("the App") · Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Summary

The App is a personal tool used only by its Operator, Daniel, to publish the Operator's own videos to the Operator's own TikTok account. It does not collect data from visitors or any other users.

2. Information the App handles

  • TikTok authorization tokens (access and refresh tokens) issued when the Operator connects their TikTok account. They are stored only on the Operator's own computer.
  • Basic account information available through the scopes the Operator approves (such as display name), used only to confirm the connection.
  • Video files the Operator creates, which are sent to TikTok for posting.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate with TikTok and upload the Operator's videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for profiling.

4. Storage and retention

Tokens and videos remain on the Operator's device. The Operator may delete them at any time. Authorization can be revoked in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, after which the stored tokens stop working.

5. Third parties

The only third party the App communicates with is TikTok, subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and is not offered to the public.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest revision.
